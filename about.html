About ClueWell-Mail

What is phishing?

Phishing emails impersonate trusted senders to trick recipients into revealing credentials, making payments, or clicking malicious links. They often rely on urgency, authority, and familiar branding to bypass a reader's judgment.

Why detection matters

Phishing remains one of the most common entry points for account compromise and data breaches. Catching the warning signs in a message before acting on it can prevent credential theft, financial loss, and further compromise.

How ClueWell-Mail analyzes an email

The analyzer is designed around a pipeline that looks at an email from several angles rather than relying on a single signal:

  • Sender analysis — checking the sender address for signs of spoofing or mismatched domains.
  • Subject analysis — flagging subject lines that use pressure tactics or misleading claims.
  • Email body analysis — scanning content for suspicious phrasing and credential requests.
  • URL analysis — inspecting links for suspicious domains or masking.
  • Risk scoring — combining signals into an overall risk score and confidence level.
  • Explainable detection — surfacing the specific reasons behind a verdict, instead of a black-box result.

Current status

ClueWell Mail uses a JavaScript-based rule engine to analyze email content, sender information, keywords, and URLs to identify potential phishing, spam, or legitimate messages. The system generates a risk score and detection reasons based on predefined rules.

Note: The current detection system is rule-based and does not use machine learning or an external AI/ML backend.
